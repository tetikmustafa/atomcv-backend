Introducing Aurora Blend, the coffee that wakes up with you

There is a moment, just before the day begins, when the light is still soft and
the house is quiet. That moment deserves better coffee.

Aurora Blend is grown at 1,600 metres on the slopes above Huila, picked by hand
between October and December, and roasted in small batches in our workshop in
Trieste. The result is a cup with the body of a classic Italian espresso and a
brightness you will notice first in the finish: stone fruit, a little cocoa, and
a long, clean aftertaste that does not need sugar to be pleasant.

Every bag is sealed within four hours of roasting and stamped with the date. We
do not sell coffee older than three weeks, which is why we sometimes run out.
