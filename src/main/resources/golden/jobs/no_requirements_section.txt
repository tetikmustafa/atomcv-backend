Product Engineer

We are a small team building tools for independent bookshops. Six people, four
of them engineers, and about nine hundred shops using what we make. The work is
a mix of things: this month one of us is rewriting the stock import so that a
shop can upload a supplier spreadsheet without renaming the columns first,
another is on the till software, and a third is untangling the way we calculate
returns to publishers.

What you would do here depends on what the shops need, which is why this
advertisement does not have a list of technologies. We write mostly Ruby and
some TypeScript, we run on one database we understand well, and we would rather
you were curious about bookselling than fluent in anything in particular. The
person we hired last year had never written Ruby and is now the one who knows
the returns code best.

We work four days a week, the fifth being nobody's. We pay the same band to
everyone at the same level and we publish it: 62,000 GBP for this position. We
do not set take-home exercises; we pay for a day of pairing instead.

If any of that appeals, write to us and tell us about a piece of software you
have used that you wish had been made differently, and why.
