Senior Backend Engineer - Payments Platform
Northwind Ledger, Berlin (hybrid, two days on site)

About the role
Our payments platform settles roughly four million transactions a day for
merchants across fourteen markets. The ledger behind it is written in Go and
runs on Kubernetes; the team that owns it is six engineers and we are hiring a
seventh to work on throughput and correctness rather than on new surface area.

Responsibilities
- Design and scale the services that route, authorise and settle payments
- Own the correctness of the double-entry ledger, including reconciliation jobs
- Reduce tail latency on the authorisation path, currently p99 at 340 ms
- Take part in the on-call rotation, one week in six, with a day back after
- Review designs from the two teams that build on top of the ledger

Requirements
- At least five years writing production services, three of them in Go
- Practical Kubernetes experience: you have debugged a failing rollout, not
  only applied a manifest somebody else wrote
- PostgreSQL at a level where you can read a query plan and act on it
- Distributed systems fundamentals: idempotency, exactly-once as a fiction,
  retries and the backpressure they need
- Comfortable writing English for an audience that does not share your timezone

Nice to have
- gRPC, or another schema-first RPC you have actually run in production
- Terraform, or any infrastructure-as-code you have maintained rather than
  written once
- Experience with financial regulation in the EU: PSD2, SCA, or similar

What we offer
A salary band of 85,000 to 105,000 EUR depending on experience, a learning
budget of 2,000 EUR a year, and a team that writes design documents before code.

To apply, send a CV and, if you like, a paragraph about a system you are proud
of having made smaller.
