Site Reliability Engineer

A profitable, employee-owned company is looking for a site reliability engineer.
We are not naming ourselves in this advertisement because our current provider
does not know we are looking; the recruiter will tell you who we are on the
first call, and we will tell you our revenue and our runway on the second.

Responsibilities
- Keep a fleet of about four hundred virtual machines running and observable
- Own the incident process: page, triage, mitigate, write the review
- Move the remaining configuration management into Terraform
- Cut the alert volume, which is currently about sixty pages a week

Requirements
- Four years or more in an operations or reliability role
- Linux at a level where strace is a tool you reach for rather than read about
- Prometheus, Grafana, and an opinion about what belongs in each
- One scripting language you are fluent in: Python, Go or Bash
- Willingness to be on call one week in four

Preferred
- Kubernetes in production, including an upgrade you had to roll back
- Experience reducing cloud spend without reducing reliability

We interview in three rounds and make a decision within ten working days.
